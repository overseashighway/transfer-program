FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer every credit from my university?

Not necessarily. The receiving university evaluates your academic record, module content and its own regulations.

Is a 1+2 or 2+1 route available for every course?

No. Such pathways only work when supported by the relevant academic arrangements and programme requirements.

Which documents do I need?

Usually your academic transcripts, course outlines or syllabi, identity documents, and sometimes English language test results. Requirements vary.

Can Overseas Highway guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Admission, credit recognition and visas are determined by the responsible institutions and authorities.
